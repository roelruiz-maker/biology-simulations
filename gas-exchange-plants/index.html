Measurement notes

The numerical reading comes from a simulated dissolved CO₂ sensor. It is a dimensionless relative index, not pH or a concentration in ppm. Larger values mean more dissolved CO₂; 100 is the initial atmospheric reference.

All tubes begin fresh at the same reference. Switching conditions resets the tube. A new plant portion represents the same shoot at its starting state. No gas enters or leaves through the seal. Rates and colour responses are simplified.

Indicator reference

Yellow: much higher CO₂ · Orange: slightly higher · Red: approximately atmospheric reference · Magenta: slightly lower · Purple: much lower. These are qualitative categories, not a universal numerical calibration.
