Method reference

What you are measuring

Methylene blue is blue when oxidised and colourless when reduced. Time to the colourless endpoint is the measured dependent variable.

  1. Prepare tubes 1-5 with active yeast; tube 6 with boiled yeast.
  2. Prepare 0.0%, 2.5%, 5.0%, 7.5%, and 10.0% nominal glucose treatments; tube 6 repeats 10.0%.
  3. Add 2.0 cm³ of 0.005% methylene blue to every prepared glucose treatment.
  4. Equilibrate at 35-40°C for 10 minutes.
  5. Combine matching tube numbers and mix for about 20 seconds.
  6. Return tubes to the bath. Do not shake during timing.
  7. Record the time for blue colour to disappear; complete three repeats.
Worksheet calculation
Relative rate = 1000 ÷ mean time
Report as 10⁻³ s⁻¹.
